# Agent guidance

Authoritative project workflows are documented in `SKILLS/`. Slash-invoked
entry points in `.cursor/skills/` must extend rather than replace them.

Before adding, replacing, or substantially changing a model, read and follow
[`SKILLS/add-new-model/SKILL.md`](SKILLS/add-new-model/SKILL.md). Use
[`.cursor/skills/add-model/SKILL.md`](.cursor/skills/add-model/SKILL.md) for
additions and
[`.cursor/skills/update-model/SKILL.md`](.cursor/skills/update-model/SKILL.md)
for updates.

Before adding, replacing, or substantially changing a summary statistic, read
and follow
[`SKILLS/add-summary-statistic/SKILL.md`](SKILLS/add-summary-statistic/SKILL.md)
and
[`.cursor/skills/add-summary-stat/SKILL.md`](.cursor/skills/add-summary-stat/SKILL.md).

Use
[`.cursor/skills/configure-summary-stats/SKILL.md`](.cursor/skills/configure-summary-stats/SKILL.md)
for local summary-statistic selection without changing implementations.
